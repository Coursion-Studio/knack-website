Video Converter · Free

Convert video to MP4 on your Mac

Screen recordings and iPhone clips that won't play somewhere else? Knack converts them to MP4, HEVC or audio-only, natively.

Download Knack on the Mac App Store
How it works
  1. Drop one or more videos into Video Converter.
  2. Choose MP4 (H.264), HEVC, MOV or audio-only, and the quality.
  3. Click Convert. Files are saved next to the originals or in a folder you choose.
Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Video Converter is one of Knack's free tools.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion uses Apple's AVFoundation on your Mac.

Can it extract the audio?

Yes. Choose the audio-only output, or use Knack's Audio Converter.
