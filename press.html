Knack

20+ native Mac tools in one app — all on-device, no subscription.

Quick facts

  • What: All-in-one macOS toolkit: AI background remover, image upscaler, PDF toolkit, OCR, document scanner, audio transcription, converters, menu-bar system stats, and more — 24 tools
  • Platform: macOS 15+, Apple Silicon; 16.6 MB; native SwiftUI
  • Privacy: Every tool runs on-device (Core ML / Neural Engine). Files never leave the Mac
  • Price: Free core tools; Knack Pro is a one-time $4.99 (no subscription)
  • Developer: Kuray Ogun — solo developer, Coursion
  • Released: August 2026, Mac App Store

The story

Knack began as one developer's revolt against a dock full of single-purpose, subscription-priced utilities. It shipped after four App Store rejections and one revoked signing certificate — and every tool, including its AI suite, runs entirely on-device.
