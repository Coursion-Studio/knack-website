Tidy Folders · Pro

Clean up your Downloads folder in one click

Hundreds of installers, screenshots and PDFs? Tidy Folders sorts them into tidy sub-folders — and you can undo it with one click.

Download Knack on the Mac App Store
How it works
  1. Drop your Downloads or Desktop folder into Tidy Folders.
  2. Choose how to sort: by file type, date or size. Preview every move before it happens.
  3. Click Apply. Changed your mind? Undo puts everything back.
Questions

Will it touch folders I already organized?

Not if you turn on 'Leave existing folders untouched'. Only loose files are moved.

Does it run automatically in the background?

No. Tidy Folders runs when you click Apply, so nothing moves without you seeing it first.

Is it free?

Tidy Folders is part of Knack Pro, a one-time purchase.
