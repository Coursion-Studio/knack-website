Privacy Policy

Your files stay on your device.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Summary

Knack processes your files entirely on your device — they are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The only data the app collects is anonymous, aggregate usage analytics and crash reports, used solely to improve the app. It contains no personal data, no account, and never the names or contents of your files. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. This website counts page views on our own server, with no cookies.

What we collect

Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports — nothing else. To learn which features people use and to fix crashes, Knack sends anonymous, aggregate events (for example, that a tool was opened, plus the app version and build). This is handled by PostHog (product analytics) and Sentry (crash reporting). We do not collect personal data, user accounts, advertising identifiers, or any file names, paths, or contents. Analytics profiles are anonymous and are never linked to your identity, and we never track you across other apps or websites. All of this is on by default and can be switched off in Settings; crash reporting then stops on the next launch.

Files you process in Knack

Every tool in Knack — Image Convert, Image Compress, PDF Toolkit, OCR, AI Background Remover, AI Upscaler, and all others — runs locally on your Mac using Apple frameworks (Vision, Core ML, PDFKit, Core Image). Your files never leave your device.

In-app purchases

Knack Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the Mac App Store. Apple handles all payment, billing, and refunds. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that transaction — see apple.com/legal/privacy.

Knack verifies purchases locally using StoreKit 2's signed transaction mechanism, which runs entirely on your Mac.

Third-party services

Knack uses no advertising SDKs and no tracking networks. It sends two kinds of anonymous telemetry, both of which you can turn off in Settings → Privacy (“Share anonymous usage & crash data”): crash reports via Sentry (EU-hosted) — app version and a technical stack trace, never your files or their contents — and usage counts via PostHog (EU-hosted) — anonymous events like “a tool ran”, never what it ran on. No user profile is created and nothing is linked to your identity.

This website

We count page views on this website so we know whether anyone is reading. The counting is done by our own server, Polaris, which runs on Google Cloud Run in Belgium. No analytics company is involved. There are no cookies, nothing is saved in your browser, and nothing follows you to other sites.

For each view the counter records the page path and, if there is one, the domain you arrived from. To count a visitor once per day rather than once per page, it computes a one-way hash of the date, the site, your IP address and your browser's user agent. Your IP address itself is never written down, and the hash is deleted after two days. What remains is a daily count of views and visitors per page.

The website is served by GitHub Pages, which may log requests (such as your IP address) as part of running that service, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for instance, if we add a feature that genuinely needs network access — we will update the date at the top and describe the change. The version that shipped with your build of Knack is the version that applies to you.
